Tintog privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Tintog is a preschool learning app. On a phone or tablet the name under the icon is Tintog. The Android application id is com.kidsplayground.kids_playground. The iOS bundle id is com.kidsplayground.kidsPlayground.

Information the app collects

Tintog does not collect personal information. It does not ask for an account, a name, an email address, a location, photos, or the microphone. Lessons, pictures, and speech are stored inside the app on the device. What a child taps is not sent to the developer or to an advertising or analytics service.

Children

Tintog is made for young children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Ads and purchases

Tintog has no advertisements and no in-app purchases.

Sharing

There is no personal information to share, sell, or give to another company.

Internet

Tintog is made to be used without an internet connection. It does not transmit personal information off the device.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to ksinghrathore18@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change and this page will be updated.
